HOW IT WORKS

Three policies, one workload

FIFO

First In, First Out

Evicts the page that entered RAM earliest, without considering its recent use.

LRU

Least Recently Used

Evicts the page that has gone the longest without being accessed.

SMART

Frequency + recency

Ranks resident pages by normalized access frequency and recency. The lowest score is evicted.
